## 摘要
视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型在语言引导的机器人任务中展现出令人期待的能力。然而，使VLA策略可靠仍具挑战性，因为操作任务是通过闭环交互完成的，每一步动作都会影响后续执行。为分析这一问题，我们重新审视执行过程中的VLA策略，并认为VLA策略既充当规划者（做出改变执行方向的任务导向决策），又充当执行者（通过密集的连续动作实现这些决策）。这一观点表明，提升VLA可靠性需要特别关注规划动作。现有优化方法可以模仿动作或改进完整轨迹，但它们通常不会明确识别规划动作或衡量其对任务成功的重要性。为解决该问题，我们提出面向VLA模型的规划感知策略优化（PAPO-VLA）。PAPO-VLA首先通过联合考虑动作变化和轨迹结果来识别规划动作，然后通过因果充分性和因果必要性估计其重要性，最后将该重要性纳入GRPO优势估计。这样，更重要的规划动作获得更强的优化重点，而整个轨迹仍通过轨迹级反馈进行优化。在多个基准上的实验证明了PAPO-VLA的有效性。

## Abstract
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models show promising ability in language-guided robotic tasks. However, making VLA policies reliable remains challenging, because a manipulation task is completed through closed-loop interaction, where each action affects subsequent execution. To analyze this problem, we revisit VLA policy during execution and argue that a VLA policy acts both as a planner, which makes task-oriented decisions that change the direction of execution, and as an executor, which realizes these decisions through dense continuous actions. This view suggests that improving VLA reliability requires particular attention to planning actions. Existing optimization methods can imitate actions or improve complete trajectories, but they usually do not explicitly identify planning actions or measure their importance for task success. To address this issue, we propose Planning-Aware Policy Optimization for VLA models (PAPO-VLA). PAPO-VLA first identifies planning actions by jointly considering action variation and trajectory outcome, then estimates their importance through causal sufficiency and causal necessity, and finally incorporates this importance into GRPO advantage estimation. In this way, more important planning actions receive stronger optimization emphasis, while the whole trajectory is still optimized by trajectory-level feedback. Experiments on multiple benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of PAPO-VLA.

## 2. 方法论

### 核心思想
- 将VLA策略分解为规划者（决定任务方向）和执行者（产生连续动作），并重点优化规划动作。
- 通过因果充分性（保持该动作能否使任务更可能成功）和因果必要性（改变该动作是否导致任务失败）量化规划动作的重要性，并将该重要性融入GRPO（分组相对策略优化）的优势估计中，使重要动作获得更大优化权重。

### 关键技术细节
1. **规划动作识别**：
   - 计算动作变化幅度 \( u_t = \frac{1}{d} \|a_t - a_{t-1}\|_1 \)，归一化后得 \(\tilde{u}_t\)。
   - 引入轨迹结果门控 \( g(\tau) = \frac{r(\tau)-r_{\min}}{r_{\max}-r_{\min}} \in [0,1] \)。
   - 规划动作评分 \( s_t = \tilde{u}_t g(\tau) \)，取前k个动作作为规划动作。

2. **因果重要性估计**：
   - **因果充分性** \( C_t^{\text{suff}} = m_t^{\text{plan}} \cdot [\mathbb{E}_{\pi(\cdot|A_{\le t})} r(\tau) - \mathbb{E}_{\pi(\cdot|\bar{A}_{\le t})} r(\tau)]_+ \)，其中 \(A_{\le t}\) 保留原动作，\(\bar{A}_{\le t}\) 替换为扰动动作。
   - **因果必要性** \( C_t^{\text{nec}} = m_t^{\text{plan}} \cdot [\mathbb{E}_{\pi(\cdot|A)} r(\tau) - \mathbb{E}_{\pi(\cdot|\bar{A}_t)} r(\tau)]_+ \)，比较完整序列与仅替换该动作的序列。
   - 综合重要性 \( C_t^{\text{plan}} = \frac{2 C_t^{\text{suff}} C_t^{\text{nec}}}{C_t^{\text{suff}} + C_t^{\text{nec}}} \)（调和平均），要求同时高才视为重要。

3. **规划感知优化**：
   - 在GRPO基础上，定义规划感知优势 \( \hat{A}_{i,t} = A_i + \eta \cdot C_{i,t}^{\text{plan}} \)，其中 \(A_i\) 为轨迹级组相对优势。
   - 优化目标：\( J_{\text{PAPO}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\frac{1}{G}\sum_i \frac{1}{T_i}\sum_t (\min(\rho_{i,t}\hat{A}_{i,t}, \text{clip}(\rho_{i,t},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat{A}_{i,t}) - \beta D_{\text{KL}})] \)。

### 算法流程（文字说明）
1. 对每个任务输入 \(x=(o_0,l)\)，从旧策略采样G条候选轨迹。
2. 对每条轨迹，计算轨迹级奖励，并识别规划动作（基于动作变化和结果门控）。

4. 将重要性融入GRPO优势，更新策略参数。
